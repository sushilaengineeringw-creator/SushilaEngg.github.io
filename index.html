PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana — Official Partner

Under this central government scheme, eligible households receive up to ₹78,000 direct subsidy for a 3kW rooftop solar system, plus up to 300 units of free electricity every month. As an authorized vendor, Sushila Engineering Works handles your complete application, DISCOM approval, net metering, and installation — end to end. GSTIN: 27BNSPT7176A1ZF

1kW: ₹30,000 Subsidy 2kW: ₹60,000 Subsidy 3kW: ₹78,000 Subsidy

Who Is Eligible

  • Indian household with its own rooftop & a valid electricity connection
  • Roof suitable for solar panel installation (no major shading)
  • No existing subsidy already availed on the same connection
  • Valid documents — Aadhaar, electricity bill & bank account for DBT

What You Gain

  • Direct subsidy credited to your bank account (DBT)
  • Up to 300 units/month free electricity, subject to system size
  • Lower electricity bills for 25+ years of panel life
  • Option to sell surplus units back to the grid via net metering

Common Questions

Solar Installation FAQs — Jalgaon

How much does rooftop solar cost in Jalgaon?

Cost depends on system size, panel brand and roof type. Use the EMI calculator above for a quick estimate, or request a free site survey for an exact, itemised quote.

Am I eligible for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

Residential rooftop systems up to 3kW are typically eligible for the central subsidy, subject to current scheme rules, DISCOM approval and portal registration. We handle the documentation for you — ask us for current eligibility and figures.

Do you install solar outside Jalgaon city?

Yes — we cover the entire Jalgaon district, including Bhusawal, Chalisgaon, Pachora, Chopda, Amalner, Dharangaon, Erandol, Raver, Yawal, Jamner, Bhadgaon, Parola and Muktainagar.

How long does installation take?

A typical residential system is installed within a few days of material arriving on site, once the design and net-metering paperwork are approved. Larger commercial/industrial systems take longer depending on scope.

Can I get a solar loan?

Yes, several banks and NBFCs offer solar loans. Use the EMI calculator above for an estimate, then message us on WhatsApp — we can point you toward suitable financing options.
